# Heat, Breaks, and Hidden Damage

An interactive web page for a construction company in College Station, Texas. It shows when a typical July day is hottest, how many minutes of rest a crew needs each hour to stay under the NIOSH heat-stress limit, and why time spent overheated matters for long-term kidney, heart, and brain health, not just for heat stroke on the day.

Sliders set the day's high temperature, how hard the crew is working, the shift start time, and how much rest the company gives. Checkboxes switch between sun and shade and between acclimatized and new workers. A line graph of heat stress through the day, a bar graph of rest needed per hour, and an hour-by-hour table update as the sliders move. Every default value and formula is sourced on the page.
